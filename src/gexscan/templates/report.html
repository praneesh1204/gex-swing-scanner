Method

GEX uses the public dealer convention (dealers long calls, short puts), recomputed with Black-Scholes gamma at the current spot for expiries ≤ {{ max_dte }} days. Walls are the strikes with the largest call and put gamma exposure. The flip is where total modelled GEX crosses zero. Scores weigh regime, wall location (confirmed by the volume profile), IV against realized vol, trend, liquidity and options activity; severe news flags cost points, and earnings inside the trade window vetoes a trade. Exits: credit trades take profit at {{ (cfg_trades.take_profit_pct*100)|int }}% of the credit, stop when the cost to close reaches {{ '%g'|format(cfg_trades.stop_credit_multiple) }}× the credit (loss = {{ '%g'|format(cfg_trades.stop_credit_multiple - 1) }}× the credit) or the underlying closes beyond the short strike, and exit on time at {{ cfg_trades.time_exit_dte }} DTE or halfway to expiry. No rolling. Open interest updates once a day, so levels are prior-session estimates.
